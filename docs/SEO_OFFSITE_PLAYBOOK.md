Highest-impact backlink sources:

2. **Partner/vendor sites** — hardware suppliers, co-working spaces, startup communities.

## AI assistant visibility (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini)

You cannot register directly with OpenAI. AI tools learn from:

1. **Your site** — [`web/llms.txt`](../web/llms.txt), [`web/faq.html`](../web/faq.html), static service pages.
2. **Third-party mentions** — LinkedIn, directories, reviews, news, Reddit/Quora answers (organic only).
3. **Consistent facts** — same company name, services, and contact everywhere.

When someone asks “software company in Thrissur”, AI will cite whoever has the clearest, most authoritative presence. Your goal: be that source.

**Do:**
- Keep `llms.txt` updated when services or team change.
- Answer Quora/Reddit questions genuinely (no spam) linking to relevant VStack pages.
- Get listed on Clutch/GoodFirms with project descriptions.

**Don't:**
- Expect instant AI recommendations without external citations.
- Copy-paste marketing fluff — AI prefers factual, structured content.
